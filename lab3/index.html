About

Little Lemon is a family-owned Mediterranean restaurant, focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist.

We are a family owned Mediterranean restaurant, focused on traditional recipes served with a modern twist. We are located in the heart of Chicago, Illinois. We are open for lunch and dinner, 7 days a week. We look forward to serving you!

Our chefs are passionate about creating delicious and authentic Mediterranean dishes using fresh, locally sourced ingredients. We believe that food should not only taste good but also be good for you. That's why we offer a variety of healthy options on our menu, including vegetarian and gluten-free dishes.
